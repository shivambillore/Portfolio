Case Study

Go Nuts November — Bikat Adventures

Campaign Philosophy

Go Nuts November was built around a singular idea: "Celebrate the tribe that does hard things on purpose." November is traditionally a slow window for the trekking industry before winter sets in. Rather than leading with heavy discounts, the campaign shifted focus from "Buy a trek" to "Become part of the tribe," inviting participants to demonstrate grit through a month-long UGC challenge.

Execution & Deliverables

  • Owned end-to-end campaign architecture from naming and brand narrative to mechanics and community language.
  • Scripted and directed kickoff reels, explainer posts, narrative carousels, and meme assets across 7 weeks.
  • Structured micro-challenge prompts, daily story reshares, live polls, and weekly leaderboards.
  • Managed community communication, influencer/creator alumni outreach, and final winner announcements.
